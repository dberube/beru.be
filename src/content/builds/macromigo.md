MacroMigo is an AI-native eating assistant for making better food and diet choices without giving up satisfying meals. Take a picture of your food or a restaurant menu, then chat with Migo for help choosing, logging, and understanding what you eat.

It tracks meals and progress for you, making it simpler to build healthier habits and monitor how they add up over time.
